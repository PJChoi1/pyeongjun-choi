About

I am an Integrated M.S. & Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering & Computer Science at DGIST, working with Prof. Jeongho Kwak in the Intelligent Computing & Networking Laboratory (ICNL) at Korea University.

My research focuses on resource allocation, mobile AI, and mobile–cloud collaborative inference. In particular, I study how to jointly manage deep learning models and device/network resources under practical constraints such as heat, memory, energy, and latency.
