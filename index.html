Research Interests

Mobile AI

On-device deep learning under thermal, memory, and energy constraints, including model/resource co-scaling for mobile vision applications.

Resource Allocation

Joint optimization of networking and computing resources across cloud–edge–device architectures for latency, energy, and reliability.

Mobile–Cloud Co-Inference

Thermal-aware co-execution frameworks that split inference between mobile devices and the cloud for stable, high-performance AI services.
